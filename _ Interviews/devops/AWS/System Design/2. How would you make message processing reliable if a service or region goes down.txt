I’d make sure messages are stored durably before processing (SQS), so a service failure doesn’t lose
them. Workers can retry failed messages (SQS visibility timeout and redrive policy), while a dead-letter
queue (SQS DLQ) holds messages that repeatedly fail. For a regional outage, I’d fail over to another
region with its own workers (Lambda or ECS on EKS) and queue (SQS), and replicate the required data
there (DynamoDB Global Tables or cross-region replication, depending on the database). I’d also make
processing idempotent (application logic, optionally backed by DynamoDB) to prevent duplicate
notifications during retries or failover.
