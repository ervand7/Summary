1. 3DS Authentication - customer verifies the payment

2. Authorization - funds are checked and reserved
   → Reversal - authorization is canceled and funds are released

3. Pre-authorization - funds are temporarily reserved
   → Incremental Authorization - reserved amount is increased
   → Reversal - reserved funds are released

4. Capture - authorized funds are charged
   → Partial Capture - only part of the authorized amount is charged
   → Void - capture is canceled before settlement
   → Adjustment - charged amount is corrected

5. Clearing - transaction is sent for processing

6. Settlement - money is finally transferred
   → Refund - merchant returns the money
   → Chargeback - bank returns the money after a dispute

7. Force Post - charge without prior authorization

8. Late Presentment - transaction is submitted late. Example: You stayed at a hotel in
June, but the hotel sends the $200 charge to the bank only in July.

9. Recurring Transaction - automatic recurring payment

10. Merchant-Initiated Transaction (MIT) - merchant initiates the payment.
Example: A hotel charges your card later for minibar use after you checked out.

11. No-show Transaction - charge for missing a booking
Example: You book a hotel, don't arrive, and the hotel charges you $100.
