- Transition: Automatically move objects to cheaper storage (Standard → IA → Glacier).
- Expiration: Delete objects after a specified time.
- Filters: Apply rules by age, prefix, tags, or object version.
- Execution: S3 processes rules asynchronously, not immediately.

Common mistakes:
- Moving objects too early → higher retrieval costs.
- Forgetting old versions → unnecessary storage costs.
- Not cleaning up incomplete multipart uploads.
- Incorrect prefixes or tags → unintended changes.
