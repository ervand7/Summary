- DynamoDB manages physical partitions and scales table capacity automatically.
- A hot partition occurs when traffic is concentrated on too few partition keys, causing throttling even if
the table has spare capacity.
- Auto scaling adds table capacity, but cannot fix a single overloaded key.
- Prevent hotspots with well-distributed keys; shard a hot key when needed.

> DynamoDB scales partitions automatically, but skewed traffic can still overload a key, so partition-key
design matters.
