* Debit - you buy coffee for $5 → your account goes down by $5
* Credit - your employer pays you $3,000 → your account goes up by $3,000
* Deposit - you put $500 cash into your bank account → balance goes up by $500
* Balance - you have $1,000 in your bank account
* Available balance - you have $1,000, but $200 is blocked → you can spend $800
* Hold / Authorization - a hotel temporarily blocks $200 on your card
* Capture - the hotel actually takes the $200 that was blocked
* Refund - you return shoes → the shop sends your money back
* Reversal - a payment is canceled before completion → the blocked money is released
* Chargeback - you dispute a card payment → your bank takes the money back from the merchant
* Transaction - you buy coffee → that payment is one transaction
* Settlement - Visa and the banks actually transfer the money to the coffee shop
* Ledger - your bank's list of all money going in and out
* Double-entry - you pay $5 → your account -$5, coffee shop +$5
