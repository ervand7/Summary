Scenario:
1. Client → Lambda: Requests to upload a 1 GB file.
2. Lambda → S3: Calls `CreateMultipartUpload` and generates 10 pre-signed URLs.
3. Lambda → Client: Returns `UploadId` and the URLs.
4. Client → S3: Splits the file into 10 parts and uploads them in parallel, directly to S3 (not SQS).
5. S3 → Client: Returns an `ETag` for each uploaded part.
6. Client → Lambda: After ALL parts succeed, sends `UploadId`, part numbers, and ETags (one request).
7. Lambda → S3: Calls `CompleteMultipartUpload`. S3 assembles the file.
8. Lambda → Database: Marks the upload as completed after S3 confirms success.
9. S3 Lifecycle Rule: Automatically cleans up abandoned multipart uploads.
