### Amazon SQS
- Delivery: At least once (Standard queues).
- Retries: Failed messages become visible again after the visibility timeout.

### Amazon SNS
- Delivery: At least once (Standard topics).
- Retries: Automatic, depending on subscription type.

### Amazon EventBridge
- Delivery: At least once.
- Retries: Exponential backoff, up to 24 hours by default.
